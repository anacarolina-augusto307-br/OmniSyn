# Known limitations and Scope (OmniSyn)

OmniSyn is an open-source platform for computational microbiology, microbial genomics, and introductory metagenomic analysis. It is designed to support education, exploratory research, rapid prototyping, and reproducible bioinformatics workflows. While the platform implements established computational methods, it is not intended to replace fully validated production-grade pipelines or specialized genomic analysis frameworks.

## Sequence Analysis Modules (`analyzer/`)

- **ORF prediction is simplistic and may not fully represent fragmented metagenomic assemblies.** O buscador usa start/stop canônicos em janelas fixas; não há modelagem de genes partidos, shift de frame nem anotação estrutural.
- **Codon usage** é contagem bruta em leitura +1; não corrige viés de composição, codon adaptation index (CAI) nem expressão.
- **GC content** em janela deslizante usa passo fixo; não substitui perfis de qualidade de sequenciamento.
- **Alinhamento global** (Needleman–Wunsch) é adequado para trechos parecidos; distorce comparações entre genomas distantes ou regiões rearranjadas.
- **Tradução** assume código genético padrão (bacterial/archaeal table 11 na maioria dos casos, mas não é validada por organismo).

## FASTQ Processing Pipeline (`omnisyn_pipeline.py`)

- Parâmetros de trimming derivados do FastQC são **heurísticos** (WARN/FAIL → flags fastp), não substituem revisão manual.
- Requer `fastqc` e `fastp` instalados fora do Python; versões diferentes podem alterar resultados.
- Mock data em `data/raw/` não representa complexidade real de metagenomas.

## Reference Datasets `sample_data/`

- Fragmentos de **25 kb** de referências GenBank; não são genomas completos nem MAGs curados.
- Comparações entre solo, mar e archaea são **ilustrativas**, não conclusões filogenéticas ou ecológicas.

## Web Platform Considerations

- OmniSyn uses Streamlit as its interactive analysis environment.
- Computational performance depends on available memory and processing resources.
- Very large sequence collections or high-throughput datasets may require execution in dedicated computational environments.

The current version does not provide:

- User authentication and authorization systems
- Distributed job scheduling or queue management
- Multi-user collaboration features
- Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) integration
- Regulatory-compliance auditing or provenance tracking

## Scientific and Educational Scope

OmniSyn was developed to facilitate learning, exploratory data analysis, method demonstration, and rapid bioinformatics prototyping within microbial genomics and metagenomics. The platform provides an interactive environment for exploring biological sequences, testing analytical workflows, and gaining practical experience with computational microbiology concepts.

Results generated by OmniSyn should be interpreted within the context of the implemented methodologies and independently validated when used to support research publications, clinical investigations, industrial applications, or large-scale production workflows.

Users are encouraged to complement OmniSyn analyses with specialized bioinformatics tools, reference databases, and domain-specific validation procedures whenever higher levels of scientific rigor, reproducibility, or regulatory compliance are required.
